## Who Should I Call First for Water Damage in Royse City?

Call a water damage restoration company first, not your insurance company and not a general contractor. A restoration company stops the damage from spreading, documents everything for your claim, and coordinates directly with your adjuster. Reign Restoration handles all three.

Here is the full triage sequence:

**1. Stop the water source (you, right now).**
Shut off the supply valve at the fixture, or the main shutoff at the meter. If you cannot locate the shutoff or the water is still flowing after you try, call a licensed plumber before anything else. A restoration crew cannot dry what is still flooding.

**2. Call Reign Restoration at (214) 304-0621.**
Once the source is controlled, this is your next call. They will dispatch a crew, set up extraction and drying equipment, and begin the moisture documentation your insurer will need. They are available around the clock, every day.

**3. Call your insurance company.**
Do this after mitigation has started, not before. Adjusters want to see a documented scope of loss, not a panicked description of wet carpet. Reign Restoration produces that documentation as part of the job, which protects your claim from the start.

**4. Call a plumber if the source was a failed pipe or appliance.**
If the burst pipe or failed water heater has not already been repaired, schedule a plumber to fix the source permanently. Restoration and plumbing are separate trades. Reign Restoration handles the water damage; a licensed plumber handles the repair of the failed component.

**When to call 911 or your utility company instead:**
If the flooding involves electrical panels, outlets, or wiring that are submerged or sparking, do not enter the space. Call 911 and your utility company to cut power before anyone goes in. The same applies if a gas line is involved. Safety first, then restoration.

## What Happens When You Call Reign Restoration?

When you call (214) 304-0621, you reach a live person, not a voicemail. The first call takes about five minutes. Have this information ready:

- Your address and the best way to reach you
- What caused the water (burst pipe, appliance, roof leak, etc.)
- Roughly how much area is affected (one room, multiple rooms, a floor)
- Whether the water source is controlled
- Whether you have homeowners insurance and your carrier name

The crew arrives with truck-mounted extraction equipment, LGR dehumidifiers, and calibrated air movers. The first thing they do on arrival is a moisture assessment using thermal imaging cameras and pin-type moisture meters to map exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall cavities and under flooring. That map becomes the foundation of your insurance documentation.

From there, the job follows the [IICRC S500 standard](https://www.iicrc.org) for water damage restoration:

1. **Extraction** - Standing water and saturated materials are removed using truck-mounted or portable extractors.
2. **Demolition of unsalvageable material** - Wet drywall, insulation, and flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed. This is called "flood cuts" and it is standard practice, not damage the crew caused.
3. **Drying setup** - Air movers and dehumidifiers are placed in a calculated pattern based on the square footage and the class of loss. Equipment typically runs for 3-5 days.
4. **Daily monitoring** - Technicians return each day to record moisture readings and adjust equipment placement. Drying is not done until the readings match dry standard.
5. **Documentation and scope** - Every reading, every photo, and every equipment log is compiled into a report your insurance adjuster can review.

If the loss involves mold growth (common in Royse City's humid summers when water sits more than 48-72 hours), Reign Restoration holds IICRC AMRT certification and can handle [mold remediation](/services/mold-remediation/) as part of the same project. You will not need to hire a separate company.

Once mitigation is complete, their [general contracting](/services/general-contracting/) team can handle the rebuild, from drywall and flooring to full reconstruction, so you are not managing two separate contractors through your claim.

## Will Insurance Cover My Water Damage in Royse City?

Most standard homeowners policies in Texas cover sudden, accidental water damage. A burst pipe in the middle of the night, a washing machine supply line that fails, a water heater that ruptures: these are typically covered events. What policies routinely exclude is gradual damage, meaning a slow leak that has been seeping for weeks or months.

The documentation Reign Restoration produces from day one is what keeps your claim on solid ground. Adjusters look for evidence that the damage was sudden, that mitigation started promptly, and that the scope of loss is supported by moisture readings and photos. A restoration company that documents correctly is worth more to your claim than one that simply dries fast.
