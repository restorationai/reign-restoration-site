## Restoration emergencies common in McLendon-Chisholm

McLendon-Chisholm sits in that stretch of North Texas where long dry spells and sudden, heavy downpours trade places with little warning. The clay-heavy soils common across this part of Rockwall and Kaufman County swell and shrink hard with that cycle, and that movement works on foundations, slab penetrations, and buried supply lines year after year. Add in spring and early-summer storm systems that bring straight-line wind and hail, and the two biggest drivers of calls here are foundation-related water intrusion and roof damage that turns into interior water damage before anyone notices the shingles are gone. Summer heat and humidity also mean a water loss that sits even a day or two can turn into a mold problem fast.

## Services we provide in McLendon-Chisholm

Water damage restoration here often starts below the surface, a slab crack or a shifted foundation line lets water track under flooring where it's invisible until the smell shows up or a floor starts to feel soft. We extract standing water, pull wet flooring and baseboards where needed, and dry the structure using moisture mapping so we're not guessing when it's actually dry.

Fire damage restoration covers everything from a contained kitchen fire to a total loss, including the soot and smoke odor that travels through HVAC ductwork into rooms that never saw flame. We handle structural cleaning, contents, and coordination with your adjuster on scope.

Storm damage response addresses wind-driven roof damage, downed-tree impacts, and the water intrusion that follows a compromised roof line, including emergency tarping to stop additional loss while repairs are scheduled.

Mold remediation deals with the growth that shows up in crawlspaces, behind baseboards, and inside wall cavities after a slow leak or a storm event goes unaddressed, with containment and removal scoped to the affected materials.
