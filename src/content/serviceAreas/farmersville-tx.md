## Restoration emergencies common in Farmersville

Farmersville sits in the Blackland Prairie region of North Texas, where heavy clay soil swells after rain and shrinks hard during the dry months. That movement stresses slab foundations and underground plumbing year after year, which is a big reason this part of Collin County sees so many slab leaks and cracked drain lines. Add in Texas's swing from triple-digit summer heat to sudden hard freezes, and you get two predictable failure points: AC condensate and supply-line breaks in July and August, and burst pipes when a cold snap catches an older home with exposed plumbing in an exterior wall or unconditioned crawlspace. Spring and early summer bring the region's severe thunderstorm season, with hail and straight-line wind that can open up a roof or push water past flashing that was never built for sideways rain.

Farmersville's older core includes homes and commercial buildings dating back to the early and mid-1900s, many on pier-and-beam foundations with wood framing, alongside newer slab-on-grade construction built as the town has grown.
