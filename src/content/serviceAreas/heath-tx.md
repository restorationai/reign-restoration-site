## Restoration emergencies common in Heath

Heath's housing stock sits on North Texas blackland prairie clay, soil that swells when saturated and shrinks hard during drought. That movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them, which is why slab leaks and foundation-related water intrusion show up more here than in areas with sandier or rockier ground. Add in the region's spring and early-summer severe thunderstorm season, frequent hail, and the occasional ice event in winter, and you get a steady mix of storm-driven roof and window damage alongside plumbing failures. Humidity through the warmer months means any water event that isn't dried quickly gives mold a real head start, especially behind baseboards and inside wall cavities where airflow is poor.
