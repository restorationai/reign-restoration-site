## Restoration emergencies common in Union Valley

Union Valley sits in Hunt County's blackland prairie, where the clay-heavy soil swells with spring rain and shrinks hard during late-summer dry spells. That cycle stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through or under them, which is a common source of slow leaks that go unnoticed until flooring or drywall starts showing damage. North Texas also sees fast-moving spring and early-summer storm systems that bring hail and heavy rain in short bursts, the kind that overwhelm gutters and roof flashing faster than they overwhelm drainage ditches. Because a lot of the housing stock here is rural or semi-rural, many properties run on well water and septic systems rather than municipal lines, which changes how a water loss or sewage backup needs to be handled and documented.

## Services we provide in Union Valley

Water damage in Union Valley often traces back to slab movement, an aging supply line, or a roof leak that's been slowly feeding moisture into the attic insulation and ceiling drywall. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from framing and subfloor, and dry the structure to the IICRC S500 standard rather than just running a few fans until the carpet feels dry on top.

Fire and smoke damage calls in this area frequently involve rural structures with wood-frame construction and, in some cases, outbuildings or barns close to the main house, which means smoke and soot can travel further than the fire itself. We handle soot removal, odor control, and content cleaning alongside the structural work.

Mold remediation becomes a concern anytime a leak sits for more than a day or two, especially in the humid stretches of a Texas summer. We identify the moisture source first, contain the affected area, and remove compromised material before it has a chance to spread into wall cavities.

Storm damage, from hail-pocked roofing to wind-driven water intrusion around windows and eaves, is something we see on a seasonal basis across Hunt County, and biohazard cleanup, for situations ranging from sewage backup to trauma scenes, is handled with the same documentation and containment standards regardless of where the property sits.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Union Valley

Much of Union Valley and the surrounding unincorporated parts of Hunt County mix older farmhouse-style construction with newer site-built and manufactured homes, many on slab-on-grade or pier-and-beam foundations. Pier-and-beam crawlspaces are worth checking after any water event, since moisture can sit underneath a home for days without being visible from inside. Plumbing vintage varies widely, from older galvanized supply lines in long-standing properties to PEX in more recent builds, and galvanized pipe is where we most often see corrosion-driven leaks start. The blackland clay soil common across this part of the county expands and contracts enough to shift slab foundations over time, which is a factor in both plumbing failures and the hairline cracks that let water find its way in. Structural repair or rebuild work on a property in unincorporated Hunt County typically runs through the county's permitting office rather than a city building department, which is worth confirming early if a loss is severe enough to require more than drying and cosmetic repair.
