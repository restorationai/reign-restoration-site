## Restoration emergencies common in Poetry

Poetry sits in the Blackland Prairie region of Hunt County, where heavy clay soil swells and shrinks hard with North Texas's swing between spring downpours and summer drought. That movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them, and it's a big reason we see slow leaks under slabs and cracked drain lines that go unnoticed until flooring starts cupping or a baseboard smells musty.
